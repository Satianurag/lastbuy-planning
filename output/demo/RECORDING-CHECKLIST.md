## Make a short screen-and-microphone test

Press **Shift–Command–5**. Choose **Record Entire Screen** or **Record Selected Portion**. A selected portion must include every app/PDF view you intend to show. In **Options**, select your microphone, choose a save location, and optionally enable **Show Mouse Clicks** and a short start timer. If available, **SDR (Most Compatible)** is the H.264 capture option. Click **Record**. Stop from the menu bar or with **Command–Control–Esc**. These are Apple's documented controls. [Apple: record the screen on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102618)

Record 10–15 seconds of your opening and one source-dialog click. Play it back with headphones: your voice should be audible from the first word, clear, and free of echo; the quote should be readable. Confirm that the correct microphone was selected if the recording is silent. This demo needs your narration, so no background music or system-audio setup is necessary.

Now reset the tabs, record the full take, and stop immediately after the closing sentence. You create the recording and perform any edits yourself: the [contest rules](https://founderz.com/agentathon-terms/) require participant-owned filming, editing and video design. The provided materials are preparation notes.

## Finish and verify the actual file

Keep an untouched original. Minimal editing is enough: trim the empty beginning and end, retaining the actual interactions and their context. In QuickTime Player use **Edit → Trim**, adjust the yellow handles, then save the finished copy. [Apple: trim a movie](https://support.apple.com/en-sg/guide/quicktime-player/qtpf2115f6fd/mac)

- **Duration:** open the finished file in QuickTime, reveal the playback controls and check the total duration. Aim for **2:55**, with all titles and ending frames included; the limit is **3:00**. Play the entire finished file once at normal speed, including the first and last words.
- **Size:** select that same finished file in Finder and press **Command–I**. Check **Size**, including the byte count: use **150,000,000 bytes or less** for the 150 MB cap. Leave some margin rather than relying on a rounded “150 MB” display.
- **Picture and sound:** check that the recorded Foundry details, **4,800 / 6,000** distinction, blocked approval and recovery readout remain readable. Verify voice audio throughout. QuickTime's **Window → Show Movie Inspector** also exposes video/audio format and data-size details. [Apple: inspect a media file](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/guide/quicktime-player/qtp65619118c/mac)
- **If oversized:** try QuickTime **File → Export As → 1080p**, with **Greater Compatibility / H.264** when offered. If still too large, try **720p** and inspect text again. QuickTime exports a **.mov** file; changing its extension does not convert it to MP4. No resolution preset guarantees a particular file size—check the new export's actual size, duration, audio and legibility. [Apple: export movies](https://support.apple.com/en-my/guide/quicktime-player/qtp20e395859/mac)

Use a clear filename such as `LastBuy-Agentathon-Final.mov`. The ZIP is a handover bundle, not the video or supporting-document upload. Keep the original recording and the submission confirmation. The official video limits are **three minutes and 150 MB**.
