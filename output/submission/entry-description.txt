LastBuy: every unit justified before the final order

LastBuy helps a thermal-camera manufacturer's service supply-chain director verify a final semiconductor purchase before it becomes non-cancelable. Four scoped Microsoft Foundry specialists examine engineering applicability, component-specific service obligations, physical supply and supplier commitment. Quoted source facts feed a deterministic allocation solver. Four distinct human roles approve the same source and plan version; an isolated exporter rechecks authority and reconciles uncertain writes. In the example enterprise case, 22,000 units of protected demand require a 10,000-unit purchase, a $640,000 modeled commitment difference at an assumed $80 unit price. Completed Foundry executions and real Azure integration tests support the demonstration. Testing exposed a revealing error: the model's summary said 4,800 current units, but its structured purchase input selected 6,000 historical units. The current evidence gate rejects that captured response and prevents approval/export. A customer pilot will measure planner effort and service coverage, targeting at least 50% less active reconciliation time. Enterprise records and ERP are examples; dated manufacturer and distributor references substantiate the real problem category.
